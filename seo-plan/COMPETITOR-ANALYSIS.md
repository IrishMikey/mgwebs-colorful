# Competitor Analysis: MGWebs
Date: 2026-10-22

No paid rank-tracking/keyword-volume API was available for this pass (no DataForSEO/GSC connected). This is a qualitative landscape scan via search, intended to be refreshed with real traffic/keyword data once Search Console is live (see `IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md`, Phase 1).

MGWebs operates in two markets that don't share an audience or search language, so they're tracked separately.

## Market 1: Tenerife (Spanish — "diseño web tenerife" and variants)

| Competitor | Type | Apparent Positioning | Notable Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tony Reise** | Freelancer | Web design + SEO positioning, personal brand | Already visible for the core keyword; freelancer-to-freelancer is the closest direct comparison to MGWebs |
| **docemasuna** | Small agency | "15+ years, custom pages for companies across the island" | Longevity/trust signal, local tenure |
| **Prismalia** | Agency | "Adapted to each business, exclusive designs" | Already claims a craft/custom angle — closest messaging overlap |
| **Efecto Newton** | Agency | "Fast and optimized websites" | Performance-led pitch |
| **Akrolih** | Agency (founded 2021, team with 15+ yrs experience) | Broader digital services | Team depth vs. solo operators |
| **Webintenerife** | Agency (since 2014) | Flexible/dynamic page creation | Tenure |
| **dvesign** | Freelancer collective | Bundled web design + hosting + domains + SEO | Full-stack-service bundling |
| **SEOLife** | Agency | Web design positioned under an SEO-services brand | SEO-first framing |

**Gap:** every one of these leans on "custom," "exclusive," "adapted to your business" — language that AI website builders now use in their own marketing too. None stake a position on *how* the site gets made (human vs. AI-assisted-to-the-point-of-generic) or push bilingual (EN/ES) service as a specific advantage for property owners with international guests — which is exactly the Casa Magic / Villa Chloé / Cloud Nine client profile MGWebs already has.

## Market 2: Ireland (English — "web design Ireland" / "web developer [county]")

| Competitor | Type | Apparent Positioning | Notable Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Logicode Web Design** | Freelancer/small studio (Cavan) | Web design + e-commerce | Regional/local SEO foothold |
| **Johny.ie** | Small agency (Kildare) | Custom sites, SEO, e-commerce for SMBs | Breadth of services |
| **Cloveloop** | Studio (Dublin) | Fast, performance-focused static sites | Clear technical differentiator (speed) |
| **Web Wizard** | Agency | Bespoke, SEO-friendly, SMB/startup-focused | Startup-friendly framing |
| **Webie** | Studio (Waterford) | Full-service for SMBs, regional award-winner | Local credibility (Irish Enterprise Awards) |

**Pricing landscape:** sub-€25/hr freelance/bundled models up to €45–90/hr for more established shops — MGWebs isn't forced into a race-to-the-bottom price position; there's room above the cheapest freelancers if the "real, not AI slop" positioning is credible and backed by visible case-study specificity.

**Gap:** same pattern as Tenerife — competitors differentiate on service breadth, speed, or regional trust, not on an explicit human-vs-AI-made distinction. "Webie" and "Cloveloop" are the closest tonal matches (small, personal, technical) and are the two worth watching most closely as the positioning rolls out.

## Cross-Market Takeaway

MGWebs is small enough, in both markets, to win on being specific and visibly handmade rather than trying to out-rank established multi-person agencies on broad terms like "web design [city]". The realistic SEO path is:
1. Own long-tail, intent-specific terms (e.g., "holiday rental website Tenerife," "website for beauty salon Tenerife," "custom website Ireland small business") where competition is thinner.
2. Lead with the "not AI slop" framing as a wedge — it's currently unclaimed by any competitor found in either market.
3. Use bilingual EN/ES capability as a structural advantage the single-market competitors don't have.
